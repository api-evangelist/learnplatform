# LearnPlatform

> LearnPlatform by Instructure is an edtech effectiveness system for K-12 school districts. It inventories the education technology students and teachers actually use, runs rapid-cycle evaluations of impact, issues ESSA Evidence Badges, and manages request, approval, and student-data-privacy workflows. LearnPlatform Inc. was a Techstars portfolio company and was acquired by Instructure in January 2023.

Generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline on 2026-07-19 from apis.yml and the artifacts in this repository. Source of truth: https://learnplatform.com/

## Important

LearnPlatform publishes **no public developer API, OpenAPI definition, SDK, or CLI**. Instructure's public developer program at https://developerdocs.instructure.com/ covers Canvas LMS, Data Access Platform, Elevate, Mastery Connect and related services — it does not document a LearnPlatform API. Do not assume an API endpoint exists; there is no published one to call.
